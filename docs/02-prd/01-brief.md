# Brief

What Kindred is, who it is for, and the aesthetic. Requirements per surface live in the numbered docs that follow; the design tokens are [02-design-system.md](02-design-system.md)'s concern.

## Intent

Kindred is a compatibility app for the people already in your life. You fill out a questionnaire about yourself, connect your friends, and reveal how compatible you are with each of them across the dimensions that actually predict a good relationship. The unit is the pair, not the crowd: the product's core act is revealing a two-person compatibility insight.

**Kindred is a working name.** The concept is a friends-and-dating compatibility layer, not an events or meetup product; earlier meetup framing is dropped.

## Audience

Adults in their 30s and 40s looking for quality connection: close friendships first, and dating where it fits. This audience is past the swipe-volume phase and wants depth, discretion, and signal over noise. The design reflects that: calm, editorial, and unhurried, not gamified.

## Aesthetic

Warm editorial. An ivory-and-ink base with a terracotta accent, an editorial serif for display over a clean sans for body. Reference the source screens ([222.place](https://222.place)) for the questionnaire and compatibility interaction patterns only; the color language is deliberately our own, tuned warmer and more grown-up than the source's cool green.
